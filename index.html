How to Play

Goal

Be the first to collect 3 complete property sets!

Each Turn

  • Draw 2 cards (or 5 if your hand is empty)
  • Play up to 3 cards
  • End with 7 or fewer cards in hand

Card Types

  • Properties - Collect sets to win
  • Money - Bank for payments
  • Actions - Special effects
  • Rent - Charge other players
  • Wild - Use as any color

Keyboard Shortcuts

D = Draw, P = Play, B = Bank, E = End Turn, 1-9 = Select card
